OneMan map data snapshot: 2026-10-17
Coordinates cover RuneLite world-map farming sites; adjoining plots share one site marker. STASH coordinates are read from the installed RuneLite STASHUnit API; stash-tiers.tsv maps its stable enum names to clue tiers. Added human-readable place names and split mixed farming sites into type filters.
